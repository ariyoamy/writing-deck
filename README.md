# Writing Deck

A small, distraction-free writing app for journals, fragments and commonplace notes.

> **Status:** Currently in personal testing.  
> I'm using the app day to day for a while before uploading a public build/source release.

![Writing Deck screenshot](writing-deck-screenshot.png)

## Why I made this

I've always been really interested in writing decks, but I didn't particularly want to buy an entirely new device just to have one.

There are obviously plenty of ways to make a distraction-free writing setup, but what I wanted was something very specific: a standalone app where I could keep my journal entries, bits of my own writing and all the passages and quotes I collect in one place.

My Notes app is already full of everything else in my life and I don't have a particularly good organisation system for it, so I wanted Writing Deck to be its own little space.

Everything is stored locally on my computer and I can make backups or export my writing whenever I want.

## What it does

Writing Deck is split into three main sections:

**Journal**  
A daily journal with a calendar, including the ability to make multiple timestamped entries on the same day.

**Fragments**  
A place for my own writing, poems, unfinished ideas, paragraphs, short pieces, or anything else I want to keep.

**Commonplace**  
A commonplace notebook for quotes, passages, speeches, lyrics, excerpts, or basically any bit of writing that I especially like and want to remember.

Fragments and Commonplace entries can also be tagged and searched so I don't just end up recreating the same organisational problem I had in my Notes app.

## The general idea

I made Writing Deck specifically around my own wants and habits.

Because of that, there are probably features that would seem obvious to other people that simply aren't here because I personally didn't want or need them.

The goal is for it to stay as distraction-free as possible. There isn't a giant word-processing toolbar or loads of formatting options. For the actual writing side of things, I mostly just wanted:

- bold
- italics
- highlight
- simple organisation
- search and tags
- backups and exports

And that's about it.

I don't really want Writing Deck to turn into another complicated productivity system. It's just meant to be a quiet place to write and collect words.

## Current status

Writing Deck is currently working as a macOS app and I'm using it as my everyday version, but I'm still testing it before putting a public download here.

I'm mainly checking things like:

- autosaving
- backups and exports
- long-term database stability
- journal/calendar behaviour
- tags and search
- general macOS behaviour
- access from my phone through my private setup

Once I'm happy that I haven't overlooked anything annoying, I'll start putting the actual app/source here.

## Privacy/storage

This app is designed to be local-first.

My own version stores its live writing database locally on my Mac, with separate backup and export folders. There are no accounts, analytics, or online services required for the writing itself.

More detailed installation and storage information will be added when the public version is ready.
